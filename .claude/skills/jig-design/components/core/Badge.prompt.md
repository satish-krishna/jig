One-liner: A compact status/metadata pill — use for states (Active, Draft), counts, or short tags next to a title.

```jsx
<Badge>Stable</Badge>
<Badge variant="secondary">v0.4.0</Badge>
<Badge variant="destructive">Failing</Badge>
<Badge variant="outline">thick client</Badge>
```

Variants: `default` (solid primary), `secondary` (muted), `destructive` (solid), `outline` (bordered). Keep the text to one or two words.
